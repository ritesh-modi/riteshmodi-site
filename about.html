About me

I make hard ideas feel easy.

And easy to touch. I turn dense technical topics into things you can actually understand by playing with them.

I'm the Head of AI at MarketOnce, where I lead the design and deployment of production generative AI. Before that I spent a decade at Microsoft as a Principal Engineer building Gen AI and the AI/ML EMEA Community Lead, and I was a Microsoft Regional Director from 2018 to 2020. I've spent years shipping data and AI systems, and just as long trying to explain them clearly.

Most technical writing tells you about an idea. I'd rather hand you the idea and let you push on it: change a variable, drag a value, break it, and watch what happens. That is what this site is, a growing shelf of explorable explanations in the tradition of interactive papers and playable demos.

Away from the keyboard I write books, speak at conferences, and mentor engineers moving into AI and cloud.
